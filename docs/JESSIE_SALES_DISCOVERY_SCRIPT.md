# Jessie Sales Discovery Script

**Purpose:** Qualify fit, scope configuration, and set accurate expectations.
**Tone:** Consultative, not pushy. Listen more than talk.
**Duration:** 30–45 minutes
**Output:** Completed discovery → configuration proposal → timeline

---

## Opening (3 min)

> "Thanks for taking the time. I want to understand your current front-desk flow, where the gaps are, and whether Jessie is the right fit. No pressure — if it's not a match, I'll tell you. Fair?"

---

## Section 1: Call Volume & Pain (8 min)

| Question | Why It Matters | Listen For |
|----------|----------------|------------|
| "How many inbound calls do you get per day/week?" | Sizing; ROI calc | Volume tier; peak hours |
| "What percentage go to voicemail or are missed?" | Pain quantification | >20% = strong pain |
| "What's the cost of a missed call?" | Value anchor | New client LTV; referral value |
| "When are you busiest? When are you closed?" | After-hours need | Nights/weekends/lunch |
| "Do you have a dedicated front desk person?" | Staffing context | Solo = high need; team = augmentation |
| "What happens when front desk is busy/out?" | Overflow handling | Current gap Jessie fills |

---

## Section 2: Common Caller Questions (7 min)

| Question | Why It Matters | Listen For |
|----------|----------------|------------|
| "What are the top 5 questions callers ask?" | Knowledge base scope | Hours, location, insurance, services, pricing |
| "Do callers ask clinical questions?" | Clinical boundary | Must escalate; Jessie doesn't advise |
| "Do you get spam/sales calls?" | Filtering need | Jessie can screen |
| "What questions do you wish callers *would* ask?" | Lead qualification | Services, insurance, availability |

> **Action:** Note each Q&A for knowledge base seeding.

---

## Section 3: Services & Scheduling (7 min)

| Question | Why It Matters | Listen For |
|----------|----------------|------------|
| "What services do you offer?" | Service list for KB | Service names + descriptions |
| "How does booking work today?" | Current workflow | Phone, portal, email, paper |
| "Do you use a calendar system?" | Integration scope | Google, Outlook, Calendly, EHR, none |
| "What appointment types exist?" | Scheduling detail | Intake, follow-up, telehealth, group |
| "Who manages the schedule?" | Human-in-loop | Front desk, clinician self-book, mixed |
| "What's your no-show/late-cancel rate?" | Pain point | Reminders = future add-on |

> **Key:** Jessie collects booking details; **human confirms**. Set this expectation now.

---

## Section 4: Intake & Lead Capture (5 min)

| Question | Why It Matters | Listen For |
|----------|----------------|------------|
| "What info do you need from a new caller?" | Lead fields | Name, DOB, phone, email, concern, insurance |
| "Do you verify insurance before first appt?" | Workflow | Jessie collects; you verify |
| "What's your new-client conversion rate?" | Baseline metric | Track improvement |
| "Where do leads go today?" | Handoff target | Email, EHR, spreadsheet, paper |

---

## Section 5: Transfer & Escalation (5 min)

| Question | Why It Matters | Listen For |
|----------|----------------|------------|
| "When should a caller reach a human immediately?" | Emergency rules | Crisis, billing dispute, clinical question |
| "Who takes those calls?" | Escalation contacts | On-call clinician, billing manager, owner |
| "Do you have after-hours coverage now?" | After-hours config | Answering service, voicemail, none |
| "What's your preferred fallback?" | Voicemail vs callback | Client preference |

---

## Section 6: Current Tech Stack (5 min)

| Question | Why It Matters | Listen For |
|----------|----------------|------------|
| "What phone system do you use?" | Telephony integration | Twilio, RingCentral, Vonage, analog, cell |
| "Can you port your number?" | Porting timeline | 2–4 weeks typical |
| "What EHR/PM system?" | Future integration | SimplePractice, TherapyNotes, Athena, custom |
| "Do you use CRM?" | Lead push target | HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, none |
| "Any existing AI/chatbot?" | Replacement vs add-on | Intercom, Drift, custom, none |

---

## Section 7: Compliance & Privacy (3 min)

| Question | Why It Matters | Listen For |
|----------|----------------|------------|
| "Do you handle PHI?" | HIPAA scope | Yes = BAAs required |
| "Any regulatory requirements?" | State/federal | 42 CFR Part 2, state privacy laws |
| "Who is your compliance officer?" | Sign-off path | Legal review needed? |

---

## Section 8: Timeline & Budget (5 min)

| Question | Why It Matters | Listen For |
|----------|----------------|------------|
| "When would you ideally go live?" | Urgency | ASAP = fast-track; 90 days = normal |
| "What's your budget range for this?" | Tier fit | Starter/Professional/Business |
| "Who signs off?" | Decision maker | Owner, office manager, IT |
| "Any procurement/security review?" | Timeline buffer | 2–8 weeks for enterprise |

---

## Closing (3 min)

> "Based on what you've told me, here's what I'm hearing:
> - **Pain:** [missed calls / after-hours / lead loss]
> - **Volume:** [X calls/day, Y% missed]
> - **Fit:** Jessie handles [FAQs, intake, routing] today; [booking, transfer] are detail-collection with human confirm
> - **Timeline:** [X weeks to launch]
>
> **Next step:** I'll send a configuration proposal with scope, timeline, and pricing by [date]. We'll schedule a 30-min review. Sound good?"

## Red Flags (Pause or Disqualify)

- ❌ Wants fully automated booking with calendar sync **today**
- ❌ Needs live call transfer **today**
- ❌ Requires HIPAA compliance **without** BAA willingness
- ❌ Wants Jessie to give clinical/medical/legal advice
- ❌ No one owns the phone number / cannot port
- ❌ Budget < Starter tier minimum
- ❌ Timeline < 2 weeks (rush = quality risk)
- ❌ Decision maker not involved

---

## Green Flags (Strong Fit)

- ✅ High missed-call rate (>20%) or after-hours gap
- ✅ Clear, documented FAQs (or willing to create)
- ✅ Simple scheduling (1–3 appointment types)
- ✅ Willing to do human-in-loop for booking/transfer
- ✅ Has Twilio or can provision quickly
- ✅ Compliance-aware; has legal counsel
- ✅ Decision maker on call or identified
- ✅ Timeline 3–8 weeks; budget aligned

---

## Post-Discovery Deliverables

1. **Configuration Proposal** (within 48 hrs) — Scope, tiers, timeline, pricing
2. **Knowledge Base Template** — Pre-filled with discovered Q&A
3. **Onboarding Kickoff Invite** — If they're ready to proceed
4. **Discovery Notes in CRM** — Tagged for implementation handoff
